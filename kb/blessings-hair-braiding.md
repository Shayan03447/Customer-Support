## 5. FAQ

**Q: Where are you located?**  
A: We're at 923 East Broadway, Long Beach, CA 90802.

**Q: What are your hours?**  
A: We're available 24/7. Appointments can be booked at any time, including same-day.

**Q: Do you accept walk-ins?**  
A: Yes. Customers with a paid deposit appointment get priority over walk-ins.

**Q: Is hair included?**  
A: Yes — braiding hair is included in the listed service prices.

**Q: Can I bring my own hair?**  
A: Yes.

**Q: Do you provide synthetic or human hair?**  
A: Yes. Synthetic hair is $20 per pack and human hair is $60 per pack.

**Q: Do you do kids' hair?**  
A: Yes. There's no specific age limit. Hair should be about 3–4 inches long at minimum. For exact kids' style prices, use the approved price list — if it's not in this KB, escalate.

**Q: Do you work with alopecia?**  
A: Yes, we work with alopecia clients. For specific recommendations, a team member can follow up.

**Q: How much is the deposit?**  
A: $30. It goes toward your total. We accept cash, Zelle, or card when booking through the website. For electronic deposit, please send a screenshot.

**Q: When is my appointment fully reserved?**  
A: Only after the $30 deposit is paid and verified. Until then it's deposit pending — not fully reserved.

**Q: Do you have a new-customer discount?**  
A: Yes — 10% off the first service for new customers.
