## 5. FAQ

**Q: Where are you located?**  
A: We have two Bronx locations: 2944 3rd Ave, Bronx, NY 10455 and 3201 White Plains Road, Bronx, NY 10467. Which location is more convenient for you?

**Q: What are your hours?**  
A: We're open from 8:00 AM to 7:30 PM. Appointments after 8:00 PM are available with a $40 additional charge.

**Q: Do you accept walk-ins?**  
A: Yes, walk-ins are welcome anytime before 6:00 PM.

**Q: Is hair included?**  
A: Yes, our base prices include synthetic braiding hair unless the service specifically says otherwise.

**Q: Can I bring my own hair?**  
A: Yes.

**Q: Do you provide human hair?**  
A: Yes. Human hair/curls are an additional $60.

**Q: How much are synthetic curls?**  
A: Synthetic curls/additional synthetic hair are $20 extra.

**Q: Do you do kids' hair?**  
A: Yes. We can service children of any age as long as their hair is long enough for the requested style.

**Q: How much is the deposit?**  
A: The appointment deposit is $30.

**Q: How can I pay the deposit?**  
A: Zelle, Cash App, or cash. For Zelle / Cash App, send to +1 (347) 216-6223 and share a screenshot.

**Q: Does the deposit go toward my total?**  
A: Yes.

**Q: When is my appointment confirmed?**  
A: Your appointment is confirmed after your deposit is verified. Before that, the slot is reserved / pending deposit.

**Q: Do I need to send proof of payment?**  
A: Yes, please send a screenshot after making an electronic deposit.

**Q: Can I reschedule?**  
A: Yes.

**Q: Can I cancel?**  
A: Yes.

**Q: Can I get my deposit refunded?**  
A: Refund requests must be handled by the owner. Please contact 347-216-6223.

**Q: What is the Boho promotion?**  
A: Any Boho Braids style is currently $200, including synthetic hair, regardless of size or length.

**Q: Do you offer appointments after 8 PM?**  
A: Yes, by appointment only with a $40 additional charge.

**Q: Do you wash hair?**  
A: Yes. Washing is an additional $20.

**Q: What if my style isn't on the price list?**  
A: We'll confirm the price with the owner before quoting you.

**Q: Can I send a picture of the style I want?**  
A: Yes. Send the picture and we'll match it to a listed style and price when possible.

**Q: I have a sensitive scalp / thin hair — can you braid gently?**  
A: Please speak with the owner about scalp or thinning-hair concerns so we handle it correctly. Contact 347-216-6223 or ask for a human handoff.

## 7. Escalate to Human

AI / CST should escalate when:

- Customer has a service / problem issue
- Refund or deposit dispute
- Style is not listed on the approved price sheet
- Price marked `UNKNOWN — ask owner`
- Custom / special request
- Complaint
- Any price that cannot be confirmed
- Exception to normal salon policy
- Sensitive scalp / thin hair / hair-loss concerns
- Promo codes not in Section 4 (e.g. old `BRAIDS10`)
- Customer asks for the owner or a phone call
- Collaboration / influencer / free-service requests

Refund requests should be directed to 347-216-6223.
